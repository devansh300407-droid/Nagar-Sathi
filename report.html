How NagarSathi Works

  1. Upload Photo: A clear photo taken during daylight helps AI and engineers evaluate damage severity.
  2. AI Feature Extraction: NagarSathi's neural network estimates pothole area, depth, and road risk.
  3. Municipal Prioritization: Reports are deduplicated and scheduled into contractor repair budgets.
  4. Public Verification: Upon repair, verified after-repair photos are published to the transparency portal.
