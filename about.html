Vision & Mission

"Together for Better Roads."

NagarSathi bridges the communication void between citizens encountering hazardous potholes and municipal authorities responsible for maintaining urban road networks. By uniting crowd-sourced reporting with computer vision, explainable prioritization, and transparent budget allocation, we turn road maintenance into an auditable, data-driven science.

Try Citizen Reporting Explore Budget Optimizer

The Problem

  • Fatal Accidents & Commuter Delays: Thousands of fatal two-wheeler accidents occur annually across Indian cities due to sudden road craters.
  • Lagged Reporting Channels: Citizens lack intuitive ways to flag potholes; physical complaints get lost in bureaucratic silos.
  • Arbitrary Prioritization: Municipal road repairs often rely on political lobbying rather than objective traffic risk and defect severity.
  • No Verification Audit: Contractors often bill for asphalt patches that wash away with the first rainfall without auditable before/after photographic proof.

The NagarSathi Solution

  • 1-Minute Citizen Geotagging: Citizens capture photos with automatic GPS pinpointing and instant receipt reference.
  • Automated AI Classification: Deep learning models analyze defect severity, surface area, and hazard grade.
  • Explainable Priority Engine (XAI): Objective scoring based on damage depth, road hierarchy, and complaint clustering.
  • Transparent Public Accountability: Open portal where every citizen can inspect contractor sign-offs and verified repair photos.

How NagarSathi Works (End-to-End Workflow)

1

Citizen Capture

Resident photographs damage; GPS coordinates & timestamp are geotagged instantly.

2

AI Vision Analysis

Neural network estimates surface defect area and flags potential duplicates.

3

Budget Optimization

Greedy knapsack algorithm assigns funds to high-risk arterial roads within fiscal budget.

4

Field Verification

Junior engineer verifies repair with post-patch photo before releasing contractor payment.

Architecture & Technology Stack

Built strictly adhering to hackathon requirements for clean, dependency-light modular development:

Frontend Presentation: Semantic HTML5, Modular CSS3, Vanilla ES6 JavaScript (Zero heavy frameworks).
GIS Mapping: Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap cartography.
Analytics & Visualizations: Chart.js canvas graphing.
Backend Target: Python Flask REST API with SQLite database (Ready at js/api.js).
AI Target: YOLOv8 Road Surface Defect Classifier & GeoPandas spatial clustering.

Future Product Roadmap

  • Public Bus Camera Integration: Mount edge AI cameras on municipal city buses to continuously survey road networks daily without human inspection trips.
  • Aerial Drone Highway Patrol: Automated quadcopter flights over expressways to detect early longitudinal fissures.
  • Contractor Performance Rating Index: Public scorecard ranking contractors on pothole recurrence within warranty periods.
  • Asphalt Sensor Moisture Telemetry: Embed low-power LoRaWAN moisture probes under prone road intersections.
